BreezingBlox Privacy Policy

Tiny Pawn · Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information BreezingBlox ("the game"), made by Tiny Pawn ("we", "us"), collects, why, and what you can do about it. The game has no ads, no in-app purchases and no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

What we collect

  • A device identifier. When you go online, the game signs you in as a guest using an identifier of your device (or a random identifier stored on the device when one isn't available). It is used only to keep your account, progress and scores tied to you.
  • Your nickname and profile choices. The nickname, sign and profile icon you pick.
  • Scores and records. Your best scores per stage and per challenge, and when you set them.
  • Gameplay statistics. When a match ends, a summary of it: where and how it ended, score, level, duration, and how it was played (pieces placed, power ups used and similar counts).
  • Challenge recordings. For challenge matches, the sequence of moves you made, so our server can replay the match and confirm the score is genuine.
  • Technical data. Our backend provider automatically records your IP address, approximate country derived from it, device model, operating system and the times you sign in.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos or payment information.

How we use it

  • To run your account and save your progress online.
  • To show leaderboards and the biweekly challenge ranking.
  • To check challenge scores and keep the rankings fair.
  • To understand how the game is played so we can balance and improve it.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

What other players can see

Your nickname, sign, profile icon, scores and ranking positions are public to other players of the game. Please don't use personal information in your nickname.

Service providers

We use PlayFab and Microsoft Azure, both provided by Microsoft, to store accounts, scores and statistics and to validate challenge matches. They process the data on our behalf under their own privacy terms: Microsoft Privacy Statement. Data may be processed on servers outside your country.

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone for advertising.

Playing offline

The game can be played without an internet connection. Progress and challenge matches are kept on your device and sent once you are back online.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. Gameplay statistics are kept for as long as they are useful to improve the game. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete its data within 30 days.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and all its data, email us at nefisto.developer@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my BreezingBlox account" and include your nickname as shown in the game. Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Brazil's LGPD or the EU's GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, and to object to its processing. Contact us at the address below to exercise any of these rights.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
